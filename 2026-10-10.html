Partial reprogramming just asked a human eye a question

On Oct 8 at Eyecelerator @ AAO 2026 in New Orleans, Life Biosciences reported interim Phase 1 data for ER-100 — an AAV2 gene therapy that inducibly expresses three Yamanaka factors (OCT4, SOX2, KLF4 / OSK, no c-MYC) in the eye, aimed at restoring retinal ganglion cell function in open-angle glaucoma. Three OAG participants received Dose Level 1 (2×10¹¹ vg) plus oral doxycycline for 56 days. No dose-limiting toxicities, AESIs, or SAEs through Day 56; adverse events tied to procedure, steroids, or pre-existing conditions. An independent DSMB recommended escalation to Dose Level 2 (6×10¹¹ vg). On Humphrey Visual Field testing, 2 of 3 participants improved at Day 56, each with at least 28% of points more than 4 dB better. Trial: NCT07290244 (recruiting; safety-first, up to about 18 planned).

The knee-of-the-curve frame: partial epigenetic reprogramming has left the mouse and asked a functional human question — restore something a patient lost, not just a younger methylation graph. Own the limits in every take: n=3, open-label Phase 1, Day 56 only; the study is designed for safety and tolerability; visual-field signals are preliminary, not proof of rejuvenation or vision restoration. The company itself says the same.

Sources: lifebiosciences.com/…/er-100 (Oct 8) · longevity.technology/…/cellular-rejuvenation (Oct 8) · pharmacally.com/…/er-100 (Oct 9) · clinicaltrials.gov/NCT07290244 · newsroom.wyde.org/…/er-100

YouTube Shorts will starve re-upload clip farms

In a TeamYouTube Help Community post on Oct 1, YouTube said it is updating Shorts recommendations to prioritize original content and reduce reach for videos re-uploaded from other creators without added value. Channels identified as primarily aggregating or re-uploading without significant changes will likely see less Shorts distribution. Original commentary, educational value, and unique editing still count; a voice-over that only narrates the screen does not, per Creator Insider cited in Poddster's Oct 8 analysis. Critical open questions for podcast ops: no start date, no aggregator threshold, no dashboard warning, and no clear rule for licensed or paid third-party clip accounts.

The safest read for video podcasts: cut and post clips from your own channel with real editorial intent; third-party clip networks are the exposed model. Sounds Profitable Podcast Atlas 2026, cited in Poddster: 89% of podcast listeners watch related clips somewhere, with YouTube the top destination at 63%.

Sources: poddster.com/…/youtube-shorts-reuploaded-clips (Oct 8) · gigazine.net/…/prioritizing-original-content (Oct 6) · kompozy.io/…/youtube-reused-content
